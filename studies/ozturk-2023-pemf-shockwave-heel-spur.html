PEMF added to shockwave for heel spur pain

Read it on PubMed

In short

Records of 75 patients with calcaneal (heel) spurs were reviewed: 35 had shockwave therapy alone and 40 had shockwave plus PEMF. Both groups improved, but the combination group had significantly lower pain (VAS) and Foot Function Index scores after treatment and at 3 months (P<0.001).

Why it matters

Supports adding PEMF to shockwave for heel pain, but treatment was assigned in routine care rather than randomized, so differences between patients may explain part of the result.
