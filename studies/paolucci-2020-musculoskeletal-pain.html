In short

This review of 21 randomized trials published 2009 to 2018 covered adults with musculoskeletal pain, most often knee osteoarthritis but also back, neck, shoulder, fibromyalgia and foot pain. It concluded that low-intensity, low-frequency (1 to 100 Hz) pulsed magnetic fields were commonly used and effective, with no reported negative side effects; results were not pooled.

Why it matters

Covers many conditions but does not pool results, and several included trials found no difference from sham, so it signals promise rather than settled benefit.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
