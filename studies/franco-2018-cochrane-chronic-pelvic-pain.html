In short

This Cochrane review pooled randomized trials of non-drug treatments for chronic prostatitis/chronic pelvic pain syndrome. In 3 trials (157 men), shockwave lowered NIH-CPSI symptom scores by 6.18 points versus control in the short term, though the benefit may not last. Evidence for an electromagnetic (magnetic stimulation) chair, from 2 small trials, was uncertain.

Why it matters

High-quality independent review: it supports shockwave for short-term relief and finds the magnetic chair evidence too thin to judge; it did not assess combined use.
