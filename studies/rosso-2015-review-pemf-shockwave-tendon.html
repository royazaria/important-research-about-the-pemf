In short

This review explains how PEMF and shockwave may act on tendons. In laboratory studies both raised inflammatory signaling molecules (interleukins); shockwave increased growth factors and collagen, and PEMF increased TGF-beta and tendon-specific gene expression. The authors found no clear clinical evidence for either in human tendinopathy and called for more trials.

Why it matters

Useful explanation of shared mechanisms; a narrative review rather than a systematic analysis, and it predates most combination studies.
