Head-worn PEMF device for chronic pain and fibromyalgia

In short

Adults with chronic pain, 17 with fibromyalgia and 15 with other chronic muscle, joint or inflammatory pain, used a portable head-worn PEMF device or a sham for 40 minutes twice daily for seven days. In fibromyalgia patients, PEMF reduced pain more than sham, a difference that just missed statistical significance (p = 0.06); no effect appeared in the others.

Why it matters

A small, short trial: the fibromyalgia signal was not statistically significant, and the authors called for a larger trial in fibromyalgia patients only.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
