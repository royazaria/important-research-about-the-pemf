In short

A University of California, Irvine study randomized 195 patients having lumbar interbody fusion (back surgery that fuses vertebrae with bone graft) to a brace that produced an electromagnetic field or a sham brace. The success rate was 92% with the active brace (98 patients) versus 65% with the sham brace (97 patients).

Why it matters

An early double-blind PEMF spinal fusion trial with a large effect; best weighed alongside newer reviews, which are more mixed.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
