In short

This overview of 10 earlier systematic reviews, all covering knee osteoarthritis and some also neck, hand or ankle, found 5 reporting significant pain reduction with PEMF and 5 reporting better function; quality of life did not improve. The authors concluded PEMF appears effective short term, but small, varied studies do not yet establish its efficacy.

Why it matters

A map of earlier reviews: they agree most on short-term pain relief, but the trials beneath them are small, varied and often poorly designed.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
