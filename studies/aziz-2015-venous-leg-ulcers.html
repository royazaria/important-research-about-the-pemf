In short

Cochrane review of 3 trials (94 people) comparing electromagnetic therapy with sham treatment for venous leg ulcers. One small trial (44 people) reported more healed ulcers, but the result was not robust; another found no difference; a third (31 people) saw more ulcer shrinkage, possibly due to group differences.

Why it matters

A neutral verdict from the highest level of evidence: current trials cannot show whether electromagnetic therapy speeds venous ulcer healing.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
