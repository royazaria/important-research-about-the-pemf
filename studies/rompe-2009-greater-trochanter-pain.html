Outer hip pain: radial shockwave outlasted a steroid shot

Original titleHome training, local corticosteroid injection, or radial shock wave therapy for greater trochanter pain syndrome

Read it on PubMed

In short

229 patients with stubborn greater trochanter pain syndrome received home training, one steroid injection, or repeated low-energy radial shockwave. Injection worked best at 1 month (75% success). At 4 months shockwave led (68%) over injection (51%) and home training (41%); at 15 months shockwave (74%) and home training (80%) both beat injection (48%).

Why it matters

A useful head-to-head comparison with common treatments, but patients were assigned in sequence rather than strictly at random (level 2 evidence), and there was no sham group.
