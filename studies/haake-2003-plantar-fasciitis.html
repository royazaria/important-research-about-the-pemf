BMJ trial: shockwave no better than placebo for chronic heel pain

Read it on PubMed

In short

At nine German hospitals and one clinic, 272 patients whose plantar fasciitis had resisted at least six months of conservative care received shockwave or placebo. Success at 12 weeks was 34% versus 30%, and pain ratings and walking ability up to a year did not differ. Few side effects were reported.

Why it matters

A major negative trial in a leading general medical journal and a key reason the plantar fasciitis evidence is regarded as mixed.
