Shockwave · Erectile function and Peyronie's

Cochrane: small, low-certainty ED benefit from low-intensity shockwave

Read it on PubMed

In short

University of Minnesota-led reviewers pooled 21 randomized trials. Short term, erectile function scores may rise 3.89 points, just under the 4-point minimal important change; longer term (5 trials) they may rise 5.25 points. Adverse events appeared similar to sham, all evidence was low certainty, and several trials were funded by device makers.

Why it matters

A rigorous Cochrane evidence summary for ED; its low-certainty findings temper the more favorable conclusions of earlier meta-analyses.
