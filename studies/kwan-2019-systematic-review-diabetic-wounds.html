In short

This review appraised cell and animal studies of energy-based treatments for diabetic wounds: 5 on PEMF, 4 on shockwave, 4 on electrical stimulation, 66 on light therapy and 3 on ultrasound. PEMF and low-level laser significantly reduced wound size versus control or sham. Shockwave findings conflicted: two studies showed smaller wounds, but one found no faster closure.

Why it matters

Preclinical evidence only; it suggests PEMF has more consistent laboratory support than shockwave for diabetic wounds, which human trials would need to confirm.
