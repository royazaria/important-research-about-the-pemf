PEMF added to physiotherapy for chronic low back pain

Year

Read it on the publisher

In short

Twenty-seven adults with long-term low back pain of no identifiable cause had 4 weeks of standard physiotherapy (heat, ultrasound, exercise) plus ten 10-minute sessions of PEMF or sham PEMF. Pain (0 to 10) fell 3.85 points with PEMF versus 1.64 with sham, and disability (0 to 100) improved 15.08 versus 4.86 points.

Why it matters

A small pilot with no follow-up beyond the treatment period; it supports PEMF as an add-on to physiotherapy but needs a larger trial.
