Early PEMF for slow-healing long-bone fractures after surgery

In short

58 patients whose surgically treated long-bone fractures were healing slowly (16 weeks to 6 months after surgery) received PEMF (31) or sham treatment (27). After three months the difference was not significant, but by study end (average 4.8 months of PEMF), 77.4% of PEMF patients had healed versus 48.1% with sham.

Why it matters

A randomized, sham-controlled trial showing a significant difference in healing rates, though small and from a single hospital.
