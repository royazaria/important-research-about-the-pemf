In short

Researchers from Sheba and Rambam medical centers randomized 58 men with vascular ED who could not have intercourse even with PDE5 inhibitor pills to 12 shockwave or sham sessions. One month later, taking pills, 54.1% of treated men versus none with sham reached adequate hardness, and 40.5% versus none met the erectile function response criterion.

Why it matters

Notable for testing harder-to-treat men, but follow-up was only 1 month and erections were measured while still using pills; the authors called for longer studies.
