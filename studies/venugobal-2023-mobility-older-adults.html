Weekly 10-minute PEMF sessions and mobility in older adults

In short

101 adults aged 38 to 91, most with mobility problems, had 10-minute PEMF sessions on the thigh once a week for 12 weeks, with no comparison group. Mobility tests improved, pain eased, and in a subgroup measured for body composition, muscle mass rose while total and deep belly fat fell, most clearly in older participants.

Why it matters

An uncontrolled study of walk-in volunteers, co-authored by the device developer's co-founder and staff: interesting, but randomized trials are needed.
