PEMF for fibromyalgia pain: small placebo-controlled pilot

In short

Twenty-one women with fibromyalgia received 20-minute PEMF sessions on set acupuncture points at the start, week 4 and week 8, or a minimal-intensity placebo on non-target points. Pain scores fell 48 points with PEMF versus about 17 with placebo, with larger drops in widespread pain and symptom severity; quality-of-life gains were not significant.

Why it matters

A small pilot with three sessions, using a device provided by a company whose employee co-authored it; promising, but it needs a larger trial.
