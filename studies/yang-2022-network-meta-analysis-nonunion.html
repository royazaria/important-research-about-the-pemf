In short

Across 30 randomized trials (1,711 patients in the healing-rate analysis) of add-on treatments for nonunion or delayed union, bone marrow aspirate combined with platelet-rich plasma or bone graft ranked highest for healing, and low-intensity ultrasound shortened healing time. Compared with control, electromagnetic fields (odds ratio 13.21) and shockwave (4.90) were linked to more adverse events.

Why it matters

Indirect comparison only; the side-effect estimates come from 10 studies with very wide uncertainty, and neither energy therapy ranked among the best for healing.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
