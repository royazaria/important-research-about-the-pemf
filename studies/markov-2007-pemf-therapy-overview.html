In short

In this short overview, M.S. Markov described how magnetic and electromagnetic field therapies, including PEMF, are used as non-invasive treatments for injuries, pain, inflammation and bone, joint and muscle problems, and how computer-based technology was advancing the devices. It offers the author's perspective rather than new study data.

Why it matters

A brief narrative overview: useful background, but it presents no new data and is weaker evidence than a systematic review.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
