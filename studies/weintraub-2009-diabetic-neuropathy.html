Large diabetic neuropathy trial: PEMF did not beat sham for pain

In short

Researchers at 16 sites in 13 US states randomized 225 people with diabetic peripheral neuropathy to PEMF or sham devices on the feet, 2 hours daily for 3 months. Pain scores did not differ; 44% versus 31% reported overall improvement, and in a 27-person biopsy subgroup, 29% on PEMF showed increased nerve-fiber density versus none on sham.

Why it matters

A large multicenter negative result: no pain relief at this dose, though signs of nerve-fiber regrowth led the authors to suggest testing stronger fields.
