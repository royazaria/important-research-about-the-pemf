Electromagnetic chair no better than sham for stress incontinence

Original titleA double-blind randomized controlled trial of electromagnetic stimulation of the pelvic floor vs sham therapy in the treatment of women with stress urinary incontinence

In short

In New Zealand, 70 women with confirmed stress urinary incontinence were randomized to active or sham electromagnetic chair stimulation, three sessions weekly for 6 weeks. Women improved on pad tests and quality-of-life scores, but active gains were not significantly better than sham; those unable to contract the pelvic floor well leaked less with active treatment.

Why it matters

A key sham-controlled negative trial: much of the improvement seen with chair-based stimulation may come from placebo effects, except perhaps in women with weak pelvic muscles.
