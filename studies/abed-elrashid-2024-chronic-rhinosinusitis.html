PEMF for chronic sinusitis: double-blind trial in 47 adults

In short

Forty-seven adults aged 19 to 40 with mild-to-moderate chronic rhinosinusitis (long-term sinus inflammation) had PEMF three times a week for a month, or the same routine with an inactive device. Fatigue and headache scores fell significantly more with PEMF, and CT scans showed more sinus clearing.

Why it matters

A small double-blind trial testing a new use for PEMF; promising, but the results need replication in larger studies.
