Adding PEMF to physical therapy for a herniated neck disc

Original titleEfficiency of pulsed electromagnetic fields on pain, disability, anxiety, depression, and quality of life in patients with cervical disc herniation: a randomized controlled study

In short

74 adults with a herniated neck disc received 3 weeks of physical therapy (electrical nerve stimulation and hot packs) plus real or sham PEMF; 64 finished. Both groups improved, but at 12 weeks only pain and sleep had improved more with PEMF; disability, mood and other quality-of-life scores did not differ.

Why it matters

A double-blind randomized trial at one Turkish clinic: PEMF's added benefit over standard physical therapy was modest and limited to pain and sleep.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
