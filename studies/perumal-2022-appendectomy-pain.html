PEMF did not ease pain after appendix surgery in a 118-patient trial

Read it on PubMed

In short

Malaysian researchers randomized 118 adults after appendectomy to an active PEMF device (58) or an inactive one (60), on top of standard pain control. Pain at rest and on movement fell similarly in both groups over 12 hours, and intravenous fentanyl use did not differ.

Why it matters

An important negative result: this double-blind trial could not reproduce earlier reports that PEMF reduces pain and painkiller needs right after surgery.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
