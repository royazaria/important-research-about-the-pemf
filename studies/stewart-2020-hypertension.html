In short

Thirty adults with high blood pressure were assigned to PEMF three times a day for 12 weeks or to a control group; 15 PEMF and 11 control participants finished. Blood pressure fell with PEMF but not in controls, and artery widening, a marker of blood-vessel health, improved only with PEMF. Nitric oxide levels did not change.

Why it matters

A small Mayo Clinic study: it hints at a blood-pressure effect, but larger trials are needed.
