In short

The review found nine studies of EMTT for fracture healing: two laboratory studies, six case reports and one pilot clinical study. Laboratory work showed bone-forming gene activity and faster mineralization; clinical reports described faster union, pain relief and better function with EMTT alone or with shockwave. Small samples, publication bias and other treatments limit conclusions.

Why it matters

Best current map of the EMTT plus shockwave literature, from an academic center; it confirms that the evidence is early and mostly case reports.
