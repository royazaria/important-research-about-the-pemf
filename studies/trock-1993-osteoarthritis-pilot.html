PEMF · Osteoarthritis and joints

Early pilot trial of PEMF for osteoarthritis, mainly of the knee

Original titleA double-blind trial of the clinical effects of pulsed electromagnetic fields in osteoarthritis

Read it on PubMed

In short

27 patients with osteoarthritis, mostly of the knee, received 18 half-hour sessions of active or placebo PEMF over about a month in a non-contact coil device; 25 completed treatment. Across six clinical measures, active treatment brought average improvements of 23% to 61%, versus 2% to 18% with placebo, and no harmful effects were seen.

Why it matters

A small pilot: promising, but too small to stand alone; the same team later reported larger trials in 1994.
