In short

This report covered 1,007 fractures that had failed to heal and 71 failed joint fusions, treated non-surgically with external PEMF coils. Success was 81% at Columbia-Presbyterian Medical Center, 79% internationally and 76% elsewhere in the US, and 75% in 332 difficult cases averaging 4.7 years of disability and 3.4 failed operations.

Why it matters

Published in JAMA, it is one of the earliest large reports on PEMF and fracture healing, though it was a case series without a control group.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
