PEMF and disc-degeneration genes in lab-grown human disc cells

Original titlePulsed electromagnetic field (PEMF) treatment reduces expression of genes associated with disc degeneration in human intervertebral disc cells

Read it on PubMed

In short

Researchers grew human spinal disc cells in the lab, triggered inflammation like that seen in disc degeneration, and exposed some to PEMF for 4 hours daily for up to 7 days. PEMF did not fully block the inflammatory response but reduced activity of several degeneration-linked genes, such as IL-17A (by 33%) and MMP2 (by 24%) in inner-disc cells.

Why it matters

A laboratory study on cells: it shows a possible mechanism relevant to disc degeneration, not a treatment effect in people.
