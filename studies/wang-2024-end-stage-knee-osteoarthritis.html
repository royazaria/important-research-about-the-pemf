PEMF plus home exercise in end-stage knee osteoarthritis

Original titleThe effects of pulsed electromagnetic field therapy on muscle strength and pain in patients with end-stage knee osteoarthritis: a randomized controlled trial

Read it on PubMed

In short

Sixty adults with end-stage knee osteoarthritis on a knee-replacement waiting list did 8 weeks of home exercise plus sixteen 10-minute PEMF or sham sessions. The PEMF group gained more knee strength and had less pain at 4 and 8 weeks (4.15 versus 4.58 on a 0 to 10 scale at week 8); self-reported function did not improve.

Why it matters

A small double-blind, sham-controlled trial: the pain difference was modest, but strength gains may matter for people waiting years for knee replacement.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
