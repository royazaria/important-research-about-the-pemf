PEMF and blood pressure in adults with metabolic syndrome

In short

In 44 adults with metabolic syndrome (a cluster of heart-risk factors), 12 weeks of PEMF (23 people) was compared with sham therapy (21). PEMF raised blood nitric oxide, a molecule that relaxes blood vessels, and lowered peak systolic (top-number) pressure during exercise. Resting pressure did not differ overall, but participants with hypertension saw larger drops with PEMF than sham.

Why it matters

A double-blind Mayo Clinic trial: it suggests a possible blood-pressure effect, but the group was small and the clearest benefit came from a subgroup analysis.
