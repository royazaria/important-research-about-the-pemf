PEMF vs laser therapy for early knee osteoarthritis

Original titleLaser therapy versus pulsed electromagnetic field therapy as treatment modalities for early knee osteoarthritis: a randomized controlled trial

Study type

Year
2023

Read it on PubMed

In short

Forty adults with early knee osteoarthritis had six 15-minute sessions over 3 weeks of either PEMF or low-level laser therapy. Both groups improved, but PEMF did better on most measures: pain at rest fell from 5.2 to 1.0 (0 to 10) versus 5.1 to 3.1 with laser, and WOMAC, a knee score, improved 19 versus 10 points.

Why it matters

A small head-to-head trial against another device therapy, not a sham, measured only right after treatment; it shows PEMF compared well but not whether effects last.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
