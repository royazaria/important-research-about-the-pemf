In short

At Rambam Healthcare Campus in Haifa, 67 men who responded to ED pills stopped them for a month and were randomized 2:1 to 12 shockwave or sham sessions. A month after treatment, erectile function scores rose 6.7 versus 3.0 points, 19 treated men newly achieved erections firm enough for penetration (none with sham), and penile blood flow improved.

Why it matters

Described by its authors as the first randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled ED study; results were measured just 1 month after treatment in men who already responded to pills.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
