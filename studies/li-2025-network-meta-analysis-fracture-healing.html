In short

This review compared seven physical treatments, including PEMF, magnetic stimulation and shockwave, across 39 randomized trials (2,379 participants). As a group they improved pain and healing, but most head-to-head differences were not statistically significant. For complete healing, PEMF ranked second (ranking score 67.6%), above shockwave (30.2%) and magnetic stimulation (23.2%).

Why it matters

A useful indirect comparison of the two technologies for fractures; the rankings are tentative because most comparisons did not reach statistical significance.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
