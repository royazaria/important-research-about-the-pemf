First sham-controlled trial of shockwave for male pelvic pain

In short

Sixty men with chronic pelvic pain syndrome for at least 3 months received four weekly low-energy shockwave sessions to the perineum (the area between scrotum and anus) or an identical sham, without anesthesia. Over 12 weeks, the treated group improved significantly in pain, quality of life and voiding, while the sham group gradually worsened; no side effects occurred.

Why it matters

Pioneering but small phase 2 trial with only 12 weeks of follow-up; later Cochrane and meta-analytic reviews give a fuller picture of this use.
