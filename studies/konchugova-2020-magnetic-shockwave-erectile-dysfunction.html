Read it on PubMed

In short

40 men with erectile dysfunction caused by poor blood flow received 7 local shockwave sessions alone or together with 7 sessions of a pulsed magnetic field applied across the head. IIEF-5 erectile function scores rose 34.1% with the combination versus 20.7% with shockwave alone, and erection hardness rose 30.9% versus 20.5%.

Why it matters

Small Russian-language trial with limited methods detail in the abstract; it is the only randomized test of shockwave plus a magnetic therapy for erectile dysfunction we found.
