In short

Johns Hopkins urologists pooled seven randomized trials (607 patients) of low-intensity shockwave strictly for ED. One month after treatment, IIEF erectile function scores were 4.23 points higher than with sham, and men starting with poor erections were 6.63 times as likely to reach good hardness. No significant adverse events were reported.

Why it matters

Supports a short-term benefit with good tolerability, but long-term outcomes were not assessed, and two authors disclosed research grants from a shockwave device maker.
