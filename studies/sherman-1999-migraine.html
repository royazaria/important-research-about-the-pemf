PEMF applied to the thighs for migraine: a placebo-controlled study

In short

42 people with migraine (34 women, 8 men) logged headaches for a month, then received real or placebo PEMF on the inner thighs for 1 hour a day, 5 days a week, for 2 weeks. In the following month, 73% of the PEMF group reported fewer headaches versus half of the placebo group.

Why it matters

An early double-blind study from a US Army medical center: interesting, but small, with short follow-up and self-reported outcomes.
