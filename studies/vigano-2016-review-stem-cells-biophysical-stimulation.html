In short

This review describes mesenchymal stem cells, repair cells found in many tissues, as a target for biophysical treatments. It reports that shockwave and PEMF can make these cells multiply, support their development into bone, cartilage or tendon cells, and change the growth factors and signaling proteins they release.

Why it matters

Explains a shared biological rationale for both technologies; based mainly on laboratory work rather than patient outcomes.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
