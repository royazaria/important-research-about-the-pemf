In short

Researchers from UCLA, Baylor College of Medicine and the University of Miami pooled seven randomized trials (602 men, average age 60.7) comparing low-intensity shockwave with sham. IIEF erectile function scores improved 6.40 points with shockwave versus 1.65 with sham (between-group P = .047), with very high variation between trials.

Why it matters

A pooled analysis limited to sham-controlled trials; the group difference was barely significant and results were highly inconsistent, so it supports only cautious optimism.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
