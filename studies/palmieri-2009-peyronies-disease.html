Shockwave · Erectile function and Peyronie's

Peyronie's trial: shockwave eased pain, did not reduce curvature

Original titleA first prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial evaluating extracorporeal shock wave therapy for the treatment of Peyronie's disease

In short

In Naples, 100 men with Peyronie's disease (penile scar plaques causing curvature) for 12 months or less were randomized to four weekly shockwave or placebo sessions. At 12 weeks, shockwave improved pain, erectile function and quality of life; plaque size and curvature were unchanged. By 24 weeks pain had eased in both groups; plaques and curvature worsened only with placebo.

Why it matters

Described as the first placebo-controlled Peyronie's trial; in line with European guidelines, it supports shockwave for penile pain, not for correcting curvature.
