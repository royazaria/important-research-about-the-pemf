Achilles tendon pain: shockwave matched exercise and beat waiting

Original titleEccentric loading, shock-wave treatment, or a wait-and-see policy for tendinopathy of the main body of tendo Achillis: a randomized controlled trial

Study type

Read it on PubMed

In short

75 patients with mid-tendon Achilles pain for over six months, despite injections, anti-inflammatory drugs and physiotherapy, were randomized to eccentric loading exercise, repeated low-energy shockwave, or wait-and-see. At 4 months, VISA-A function scores rose from 51 to 76 with exercise, 50 to 70 with shockwave and 48 to 55 with waiting.

Why it matters

Shows shockwave as a comparable alternative to standard eccentric exercise for stubborn Achilles pain; small, not sham-controlled, with only 4-month follow-up.
