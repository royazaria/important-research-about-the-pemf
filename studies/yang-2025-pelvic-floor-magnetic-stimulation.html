In short

This review covered 24 randomized trials of extracorporeal magnetic stimulation (magnetic pulses delivered from outside the body), pooling 14. For urinary incontinence it improved symptom scores (effect size 0.73) and quality of life (0.43) versus control, but it showed no clear benefit for overactive bladder or chronic pelvic pain.

Why it matters

Supports magnetic stimulation for urinary incontinence specifically; the authors say larger, high-quality trials are still needed for other pelvic floor problems.
