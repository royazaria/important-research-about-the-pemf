Pelvic electromagnetic therapy eased male pelvic pain for a year

Original titleA prospective, randomized, placebo controlled, double-blind study of pelvic electromagnetic therapy for the treatment of chronic pelvic pain syndrome with 1 year of followup

In short

At St Mary's Hospital, London, 21 men with chronic prostatitis-type pelvic pain received active or placebo pelvic floor electromagnetic stimulation (10 Hz then 50 Hz, 15 minutes each) twice weekly for 4 weeks. Symptom scores fell significantly at 3 months and 1 year with active treatment, mainly pain, but not with placebo.

Why it matters

Small but double-blind with one-year follow-up in a top urology journal; promising for a hard-to-treat condition, though the Cochrane review rates such evidence as uncertain.
