In short

A University of Warwick-led Cochrane review found 4 blinded, placebo-controlled trials (125 adults; 3 tested PEMF) in delayed union or nonunion (fractures that heal slowly or stall), mostly of the shin bone. The pooled estimate favored stimulation (risk ratio 1.96) but was not statistically significant; two trials found no pain benefit.

Why it matters

Cochrane-level summary for non-healing fractures: suggests a possible benefit but finds too little trial evidence to guide practice, a key caveat for bone claims.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
