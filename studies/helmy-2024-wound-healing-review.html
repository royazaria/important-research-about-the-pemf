In short

The authors reviewed 45 studies, mostly from 2013 to 2023, on PEMF and wound healing; most were in animals or cells, and the few human studies were small. Reported effects included faster early closure of incision wounds and, in diabetic foot ulcers, better small-vessel blood flow and shorter healing times, with benefits mainly in early healing.

Why it matters

An integrative review, not a pooled analysis: a useful map of the field, and it shows human evidence for wound healing is still thin.
