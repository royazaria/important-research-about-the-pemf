In short

This review gathered 17 studies of PEMF for osteoarthritis (16 randomized, 13 on the knee) covering 1,197 people treated for 15 to 90 days. Pain scores fell an average of 60% across 5 studies; WOMAC, a standard knee-function score, improved 42% across 6 studies, though the overall WOMAC effect was not statistically significant.

Why it matters

A broad overview across joints, but it searched only two databases and its averages carry wide uncertainty, so it adds less than placebo-controlled meta-analyses.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
