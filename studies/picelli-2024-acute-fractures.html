In short

An Italian rehabilitation society team led from the University of Verona reviewed trials from 2014 to 2022 on PEMF for acute fractures. Three randomized trials (197 patients) qualified; none found a significant effect on bone healing, pain results conflicted, and one showed a short-lived gain in strength and range of motion.

Why it matters

Recent review that does not support PEMF for speeding routine fracture healing; the trials are few and varied, so the question remains open.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
