In short

This modified scoping review searched studies from 2000 to 2023 and kept only treatments with GRADE A evidence, mostly from systematic reviews and meta-analyses. Eleven qualified, including shockwave therapy, peripheral magnetic stimulation, stretching, TENS, botulinum toxin injection and vibration. The authors present them as options within multimodal rehabilitation.

Why it matters

More favorable to shockwave than a later network meta-analysis included here; as a scoping review it summarizes rather than pools the evidence.

A plain-English summary written from the published abstract by the editors of this site. It describes this study, not any particular device or clinic, and it is not medical advice. Read the original on PubMed for the full methods and results.
