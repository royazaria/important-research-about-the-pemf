PEMF beat ultrasound for carpal tunnel syndrome after childbirth

In short

Cairo University researchers randomized 40 women with postnatal carpal tunnel syndrome (a compressed nerve at the wrist) to PEMF or pulsed ultrasound, each with nerve and tendon gliding exercises, three times weekly for 4 weeks. Both groups improved; PEMF did better on pain, nerve conduction and grip strength, but daily function scores did not differ.

Why it matters

Small trial against an active treatment rather than sham, so it shows PEMF outperformed ultrasound, not that it beats placebo; useful early evidence for a common condition.
