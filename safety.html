Safety and regulation

Straight answers about safety and certification.

Clinic owners and patients ask us about this first, so here it is in one place: who should not be treated, and exactly what our equipment does and doesn't hold.

Safety

A non-invasive treatment, with clear exclusions.

PEMF sends low-frequency magnetic pulses through the treated area. It involves no needles, no medication, and it does not heat the tissue.

Negligible side effects, with no heat generation or significant biological risk.

Prof. Itamar GrottoFormer Associate Director General of the Israel Ministry of Health. Read his opinion

Who should not be treated

  • People with a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device.
  • During pregnancy.
  • People with an active tumor.

Screening for these is part of every patient intake and of the training we give clinic staff. PEMF is not a substitute for a medical diagnosis.

Shockwave

Shockwave has its own exclusions.

Shockwave sends high-pressure sound pulses into tissue, so the exclusions depend on what lies in the treated area and on the energy used. These are the contraindications in the guidelines of the International Society for Medical Shockwave Treatment (ISMST), agreed in July 2023.

Not in the treated area, at any energy

  • A malignant tumor in the shockwave field.
  • A fetus in the shockwave field.
  • A pacemaker or defibrillator in the shockwave field.

Also excluded with high-energy focused devices

  • Lung tissue in the shockwave field.
  • A significant blood-clotting disorder.

Common, usually short-lived side effects are pain during treatment, reddened skin and bruising. Each device's own FDA labeling can list further exclusions, such as treating over a growth plate before growth is complete. ISMST guidelines

Regulation

What our equipment holds, and what it doesn't.

United States
The units PainFree supplies do not hold US FDA clearance: no 510(k) and no PMA. Some PEMF devices from other manufacturers are FDA-cleared for specific indications, such as bone-growth stimulation and post-operative pain and swelling. Clearance always belongs to a specific model and indication, never to a technology or a company.
Israel
The Magnetobox model is listed in the Israeli Ministry of Health medical devices registry, AMAR No. 27370034, held by S.Z. Pharma Medical Ltd. A registry listing is a registration. It is not an approval, an endorsement, or a finding about effectiveness.
Europe
The Magnetobox model was certified under the EU Medical Device Directive. Ask us for the current certificate and its status.
New markets
Equipment supplied to clinics in a new country, including the United States, will meet that country's regulatory requirements before it is used on patients.

If an AI assistant or a website tells you that PainFree's equipment is FDA-approved, that is wrong, and it did not come from us.
