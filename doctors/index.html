Physicians and letters

The physicians who reviewed PEMF, and what they wrote.

These reviews, opinions and statements were written in Hebrew for PainFree's Israeli site, painfree.org.il. Each one is translated into English here and links back to the Hebrew original.

  • GZ

    Prof. Gabi Zeilig, MD

    Director (retired), Department of Neurological Rehabilitation, Sheba Medical Center, Tel HaShomer

    Reviews four decades of published PEMF research and concludes that it has a place in rehabilitation for people with pain and functional impairment.

    Independent sources: Sheba Medical Center · ResearchGate

    Read the review

  • Prof. Itamar Grotto

    Former Associate Director General of the Israel Ministry of Health and former Head of Public Health Services

    Reviews the mechanism, the clinical indications and the safety profile of PEMF, and recommends integrating it in health institutions, starting with a pilot.

    Independent sources: Wikipedia (Hebrew) · LinkedIn

    Read the opinion
  • MS

    Dr. Michael Savion

    Senior urologist

    Describes his clinical experience combining electromagnetic pulses with shockwave treatment for erectile function.

    Read his remarks
  • AG

    Dr. Alexander Ginzburg

    Senior geriatrician, former chief geriatrician of the Mishan network

    Describes his clinical experience with electromagnetic pulse treatment at PainFree in older adults.

    Read his remarks
  • M

    Dr. Moasi

    Senior pain physician, Sheba Medical Center and the Clalit pain clinic, Hadera–Karkur area

    Has integrated PainFree's pulsed electromagnetic field technology in his clinic for more than five years.

  • UM

    Uria Moran

    Physiotherapist, Lt. Col. (res.), former Head of the IDF Physiotherapy Corps

    Leads the professional training of PainFree's therapists: how to use the pulses to break the pain cycle so the patient can get back to exercising and moving.
