Expert view: Dr. Alexander Ginzburg, senior geriatrician

"From my extensive clinical experience over the years, including my roles as chief geriatrician of the Mishan network and as a senior physician at the Ministry of Health, I can say with confidence that electromagnetic pulse treatment at PainFree is an exceptionally important therapeutic tool in older age. This technology supports and improves many systems in the body at the same time, without creating a chemical burden or invasive risks. Based on my experience with many patients who were treated at PainFree, its functional outcome is unequivocally good, noticeable and extremely important for preserving the independence of older people."

Editor's note: this is the clinical impression of an experienced physician, not the result of a controlled trial. The research on PEMF in older adults is in the research database.
