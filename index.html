Regulation & safety

Straight answers before you buy.

Buyers ask us about certification first, so here it is plainly — what our equipment holds, and what it doesn't.

CEEuropean certification

The Magnetobox model we supply holds a CE certificate, valid to August 2027.

ILIsrael Ministry of Health registry

Listed in the Israeli Medical Devices Registry (AMAR), registration No. 27370034, held by S.Z. Pharma Medical Ltd. A registry listing is a registration — not an approval or an endorsement of efficacy.

FDAUnited States

The units we supply do not hold US FDA clearance — no 510(k) and no PMA. Some other PEMF devices are FDA-cleared for specific indications; clearance always belongs to a specific model and indication, never to a technology or a company.

Your countryLocal registration

Before the first unit is sold in a new country, we work with the local partner on medical-device registration under that country's rules.

SafetyWho should not be treated

PEMF is non-invasive and produces no heat. It is not used for patients with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, during pregnancy, or with an active tumor. Screening for these is part of the staff training.

The PEMF equipment (control unit, treatment bed, ring applicator and local applicators), treatment protocols, staff training, the marketing playbook (templates, scripts, pricing and packages) and ongoing technical and clinical support from our team.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the configuration, the number of systems, your country and the partner model (clinic, hospital or distributor). Send us a short message and we'll reply with a written proposal — price, revenue model and timeline.

How many patients can one system treat?

Which treatments can my clinic offer?

Three lines: chronic pain and rehabilitation (back, neck, knees, joints, sports injuries), men's sexual health, and women's urinary incontinence. See the three treatment lines and the research database.

No. The units we supply do not hold US FDA clearance. The Magnetobox model holds a CE certificate (valid to August 2027) and is listed in the Israeli Ministry of Health registry. See Regulation & safety for the details.

No. You own and run the business; we train the staff who deliver the treatments. Who may operate the equipment depends on your country's rules — we check this with you during the proposal stage and help you hire accordingly.

You keep a direct line to our team by video call — for equipment questions, clinical questions and marketing. You also join the PainFree clinic network.
